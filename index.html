About

I study what happens to data when people label it with a model's help. My current work defines suggestion-conditioned label noise: the errors a human annotator inherits from an AI suggestion and confirms as ground truth. I built AILA-Bench, a benchmark of 370 annotators labeling 100,000 images with and without AI assistance, and a counterfactual per-label risk score that finds these errors better than model confidence does.

Before this I spent two years at Pony.ai as a software engineer in data evaluation, building the model-in-the-loop annotation and evaluation pipelines for a production perception stack. I hold an MEng in Artificial Intelligence from UCLA (2023) and a B.A. in Mathematics from the University of Washington (2022). I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
